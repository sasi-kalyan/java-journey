
service discovery is a mechanism where the services finds each other dynamically/automatically 
without hardcoding the hostnames/ip's. we maintain a central manager that manages/and let the 
service know (who is running where).

 <Netflix> ----> Eureka ---->(maintained by) spring cloud.

Eureka server - that is central manager that tracks who is running where.
this is the registry that stores the host, port and status of the servvices runnning where.
